About us

A craftsman's approach

Dunn Carpentry & Developments was built on the belief that great woodwork starts with listening. Every job — whether a full kitchen installation or a single fitted wardrobe — begins with a proper conversation about what you need, what you love, and what will work in your home.

We're a small, hands-on operation. That means the person you speak to at the start is the same person doing the work. No surprises, no delays, and no compromise on the finish.

Based locally and proud of it, we've helped hundreds of homeowners create spaces they love. We'd love to help you too.
