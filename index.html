Projects.

Bespoke Shelving Display Unit.

Cardiff ISG

Project Description

This project involved the full installation of bespoke curved shelving units and integrated joinery features as part of a high end commercial interior refurbishment.

Working closely with the design and joinery teams, we delivered custom-built display units using concealed fixings to achieve a seamless, high quality finish throughout the space.

Scope of Works

  • Assisted in the manufacturing of bespoke curved display units & joinery components
  • Installation of multiple bespoke display units
  • Coordination with lighting, flooring and ceiling details
  • Integration with metal framework and feature panels
  • Final finishing to commercial-grade durability and quality standards

Bespoke Timber Slatted Staircase Feature.

Birmingham BAM

Project Description

This project involved the manufacturing and installation of bespoke vertical timber slatted balustrades and architectural feature joinery as part of a modern educational facility fit out.

The slatted timber panels were precisely cut, assembled, and installed along the main staircase and upper-level walkways, creating a clean design while maintaining safety, durability, and full building compliance within a school environment.

Scope of Works

  • Assisted in the manufacturing of bespoke vertical timber slatted panels and joinery components
  • Installation of custom slatted balustrades along staircases and upper-level walkways
  • Precision cutting and fitting to angled stair runs and level walkways
  • Final finishing to commercial-grade durability and quality standards

Parkgate Hotel.

Cardiff BECT

Project Description

This project involved the manufacturing support and installation of extensive bespoke joinery features throughout the main lobby and hospitality areas of The Parkgate Hotel Cardiff, as part of a high-end luxury hotel fit-out.

Works included architectural timber wall panelling, bespoke bar frontage joinery, mirrored feature walls, curved timber doorways, and integrated joinery detailing to create a refined, premium interior finish through the public spaces.

Scope of Works

  • Installation of wall panelling and decorative timber trims
  • Installation of bespoke bar front joinery and feature timber slatting
  • Fitting of curved doorway and architrave
  • Integration of mirrored panels within bespoke joinery frameworks
  • Final finishing to luxury hospitality and commercial-grade standards

About Us

About Us.

Introduction

Dunn Carpentry & Developments is a South Wales based specialist carpentry and joinery installation company delivering high quality bespoke interiors, precision second fix carpentry and commercial fit outs.

We primarily operate as a specialist installation and labour supply team, working closely with joinery manufacturers, designers and main contractors to deliver seamless on-site installation of bespoke joinery and interiors features. Where required, we also assist with manufacture through trusted workshop partners.

We pride ourselves on delivering premium workmanship across residential and commercial projects, combining attention to detail with reliability, clear communication and efficient site coordination.
